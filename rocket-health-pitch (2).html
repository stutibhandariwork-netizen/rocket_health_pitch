05, Community Strategy

THE FOUR
PILLARS

The four-pillar system that turns Rocket Health from an acquisition machine into a retention engine. Each pillar is interconnected, together they create daily pull and lasting loyalty.

01
🛸

MISSION CREWS

Small accountability groups (10,15 people) organized by shared goal, sleep, anxiety, stress, grief. Not topic forums. Intimate crews with a shared mission arc.

  • Weekly async check-ins via voice notes
  • Mission milestones with shared celebration
  • Optional clinician-in-the-room monthly
  • Private, anonymous by default
02
🔥

RETENTION STREAKS

Gamified consistency loops that reward daily engagement, not just app opens, but meaningful actions (journal entries, Crew check-ins, session completions).

  • Streaks visualised as rocket trajectory
  • Crew-shared streaks for accountability
  • Recovery mechanics (no cold-start shame)
  • Monthly Progress Report, personalised data story
03
🎙️

SIGNAL EVENTS

Regular live programming that gives users a reason to return, not generic webinars. Intimate, human, repeatable events with real community members as hosts.

  • Weekly "Launchpad" live group session
  • Monthly expert-in-residence AMA
  • User story spotlights (de-identified)
  • Quarterly in-person or hybrid gatherings
04
🧭

CHAMPION NETWORK

A trained cohort of peer champions, users who've seen results and want to give back. Modelled on Unilever's 4,000-champion programme. Creates social proof and referral flywheel.

  • Champion certification pathway
  • Peer-to-peer first response layer
  • Internal ambassador for B2B clients
  • Champion-led Crew onboarding

06, Community Programming

ACTIVITIES THAT MAKE
PEOPLE ACTUALLY SHOW UP

Gen Z and millennials don't join communities. They join things they want to tell their friends about. The programming has to feel like a lifestyle, not a support group.

🏃 Move Together
01
🏃‍♀️

ROCKET RUN CLUB

Monthly 5K community runs in major cities, open to Rocket Health users. No timing, no competition. Just movement and conversation. Running is proven to reduce anxiety by 48%. We make it social.

  • City-based chapters, Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore first
  • Themed runs: "Run off the Sunday Scaries", "5K for your mind"
  • Post-run chai + debrief, the real community moment
  • Virtual run option for non-metro users
02
🧘

SUNDAY RESET

Weekly 20-minute live group breathwork or yoga session every Sunday morning. Designed specifically for the Sunday anxiety that hits 78% of millennials before Monday. Free for all users.

  • Live on app, no external platform needed
  • Rotating facilitators: therapists, certified instructors, peer leads
  • Streak mechanic: attend 4 Sundays = badge + community recognition
  • Recording available for async users in different timezones
03
🚶‍♂️

WALK & TALK

Peer-matched 30-minute walking "dates", two community members matched by shared goal, same city, scheduled through the app. Walking side-by-side reduces the pressure of face-to-face emotional conversations.

  • App-based matching by goal + location + availability
  • Optional anonymous mode, no last names required
  • Post-walk rating: "Would you walk again?" builds trust graph
  • Gen Z stat: 61% prefer walking meetings to sitting ones
🎨 Create Together
04
🎨

MONTHLY ART SESSIONS

Guided group art therapy sessions, online and in-person. Not art classes. Expressive art as a mental health tool. No skill required. Watercolour, collage, journaling-as-art. The output is optional to share.

  • Theme each month: "What does anxiety feel like?", "Draw your safe space"
  • Facilitated by art therapists from the Rocket Health network
  • Optional community gallery, share your piece anonymously
  • Physical kit delivery for premium subscribers
05
✍️

WRITE IT OUT

Biweekly community journaling prompts with optional sharing. Journaling reduces depressive symptoms by up to 28%. We make it a group ritual, a shared moment, not a solo exercise done in isolation.

  • In-app prompts every Monday + Thursday
  • "Share with crew" option, visible only to your cohort
  • Monthly "Letters to Myself", write now, receive in 3 months
  • Anonymous community board: best lines of the week
06
🎙️

STORY OPEN MIC

Quarterly virtual open mic where community members share 3-minute mental health stories. De-identified. No last names. Inspired by The Moth format. The most powerful community moments come from real voices, not expert panels.

  • Opt-in storytelling, never required, always celebrated
  • Live audience of community members + therapist host
  • Best stories turned into anonymous podcast episodes
  • Creates the "I could tell my story too" flywheel
🤝 Connect Together
07
☕

MENTAL HEALTH CAFÉ

Monthly drop-in community café, physical in metro cities, virtual elsewhere. No agenda. No therapist present. Just Rocket Health users, coffee, and open conversation. Safe, structured, but deliberately informal.

  • Rotating cities: one new city every quarter
  • Community-led: trained peer facilitator, not a clinician
  • Theme suggested but never enforced
  • Instagram-worthy, designed to be shareable
08
🎮

WELLNESS CHALLENGES

Monthly team-based wellness challenges for cohort groups, sleep streaks, screen-free evenings, daily walks, gratitude logging. Competitive enough to be fun. Gentle enough to be safe for mental health contexts.

  • "30 days of 7-hour sleep", tracked in app
  • "Digital detox weekends" with crew accountability
  • Leaderboard visible to crew only, not public
  • Winner: free therapy session for the whole crew
09
🌙

NIGHT OWL CHECK-INS

Late-night async voice note check-ins for users who struggle at night, 10pm to 1am window. Not therapy. Not moderated chat. Just a place to record a voice note and hear you're not alone. Designed for the hardest hours.

  • Voice note format, lower barrier than typing
  • Crew members get notified: "someone in your crew checked in"
  • Optional: auto-suggest booking a therapist next morning
  • Directly addresses the gap that kills night-time retention
